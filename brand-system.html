07 — Usage Guide

Do
  • The clay period is part of the mark — always include it in professional contexts
  • WM-1 “Plain Terms.” is the everyday mark — website, email sig, proposals, invoices
  • WM-2 “PLAIN / TERMS” is for bold applications — decks, social graphics, merch, print
  • MG-2 square is for avatars, favicons, embossed materials, and anywhere WM-1 won’t fit
  • On cream/white: ink square + white PT + clay label. On dark: clay square + white PT
  • Full legal name “Plain Terms Consulting LLC” in contracts, invoices, and legal docs
Don’t
  • Don’t add “Consulting” back to the logo — the brand is just Plain Terms.
  • Don’t use the period in a different color — it’s always clay (#C26244)
  • Don’t use WM-2 where WM-1 fits — all-caps is for bold contexts, not daily use
  • Don’t left-align the MG-2 monogram — always center
  • Don’t use clay as a large background fill — accent only
  • Don’t use blue, purple, or green — not in the palette
