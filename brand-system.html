06 — Usage Guide

Do
  • Use the clay period consistently — it’s a design element, not punctuation
  • Use “Plain Terms.” on cream or white; use white version on dark or clay backgrounds
  • Use MG-1 (PT) for profile photos, email avatars, favicons, and embossed materials
  • Keep “CONSULTING” in Inter, tracked wide, always in clay
  • Maintain clear space of at least 1× the cap height around all edges of the wordmark
  • Use Manrope 600 for all primary headlines to stay consistent with the wordmark
Don’t
  • Don’t place the wordmark on a busy photo or patterned background without a solid backing
  • Don’t stretch, squish, outline, or apply drop shadows to either mark
  • Don’t use the clay color for large filled backgrounds — it’s an accent, not a primary surface
  • Don’t use “Plain Terms” without the period in professional contexts — it’s part of the mark
  • Don’t use the monogram (PT) as the primary logo where the full wordmark fits
  • Don’t use blue, purple, or green — not in the palette, not on brand
